## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Mediheal helps you slow down and feel more like yourself — a few quiet minutes a day.

Whether you're new to meditation or coming back to it, Mediheal makes it simple: short guided sessions, calming breathing exercises, and gentle audio you can follow anywhere. No experience needed.

WHY MEDIHEAL

• A calmer mind, a few minutes a day — sessions that fit real life
• Guided programs that teach you the basics, one day at a time
• Beautiful, distraction-free player with a breathing animation to follow
• Works your way — sign in to sync your progress, or continue as a guest

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Guided meditations for focus, calm, and winding down
• Breathing exercises with a simple inhale • hold • exhale rhythm
• Step-by-step programs to build a daily habit
• Progress tracking so you can see your streak and time practiced
• Light and dark themes — easy on the eyes, day or night
• Offline-friendly playback that keeps going with your screen off

MADE TO KEEP SIMPLE

Open the app, pick a session, and breathe. That's it. No clutter, no pressure — just a calmer few minutes whenever you need them.
